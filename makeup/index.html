FAQ Common questions

How far in advance should I book my bridal makeup? We recommend booking 2–3 months before the event, especially during peak wedding season (Oct–Feb).

Do you travel for destination weddings? Yes — outstation bookings are available, with travel and stay arranged separately.

What's the difference between HD and airbrush? Airbrush is lighter and more humidity resistant — ideal for outdoor or long-duration events. HD gives a slightly richer camera finish.

Can family members book alongside the bride? Yes — ask about family and bridesmaid packages during your consultation.
